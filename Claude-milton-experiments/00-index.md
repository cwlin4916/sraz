## Headline

The derivation MDP the writeup assumes is already implemented and faithful. The
*controlled environment* it constructs is not, and cannot be reached by
configuration — the terminal evaluator parses expressions symbolically, and the
orthonormal basis functions have no closed form to parse. The Experiment-2
protocol diverges in the selection rule, the tree-persistence discipline, and
every outcome variable.

The compensating finding is that the phenomenon under study needs no
construction. On the shipped additive-quadratic instance at a 12-token budget,
enumerating all 4,898 mask-reachable sentential forms gives

```
V^q(D) = 0.983104  >  V^q(C) = 0.885023     mean-rollout ordering
V*(D)  = 0.983104  <  V*(C)  = 1.000000     true ordering
```

where `D` is the one-term expression `C1·x` and `C` is the compositional branch
holding 405,778 terminals. Pure MCTS moves its budget the wrong way as it
grows: the branch containing the optimum falls from 87.5% of visits at 8
simulations to 25% at 128, while the decoy climbs to 68%. Best observed reward
stalls at `0.998597` at every budget — simple regret `0.0014`, exact recovery
never.
