## Results at a glance

Three problem instances have been measured. **Read the status column before
quoting any number** — they are not equally solid.

| Instance | Reachable ceiling | Best cheap terminal | Search result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sine` (seed 42) | $0.9873$ @ 14 tokens (the 18-token generating form does not fit the buffer) | $C_1x = 0.8729$, **one action** | AZ locks to $C_1x$ at iteration 1, never escapes (25 sims) | Reproducible after the MCTS RNG fix |
| `additive_quadratic` (seed 42) | $\mathbf{1.000}$ — exactly expressible | $C_2x^2 = 0.929$ | AZ stalls at $0.9287$ @ 25 sims; climbs to $0.9992$ @ 100, $0.9997$ @ 400 | Reproducible |
| `lin_A–D`, `quad_A–D` | $\mathbf{1.000}$ on all eight, via the 11-token $C_0+C_1x+C_2x^2$ | varies by member; `quad_B`'s linear cheat is $0.9972$ | Pure MCTS: `lin_D` pinned at $1.0$; `quad_B` **collapses to the constant** at $N_{\text{sim}} = 8$ | Ladder reproducible. **Sweep digits are provisional** — see below |

> **Provisional-results warning.** The pure-MCTS sweep in
> [notes/02 §2.1](notes/02-target-families.md#21-what-is-switched-off) is **not
> reproducible under a fixed seed**: replaying one episode three times in a
> process yields three different sentences, isolated to `MCTS._rollout_value`.
> Its **shape** repeats across runs; its **digits** do not. The `sine` and
> `additive_quadratic` AZ numbers are unaffected — those are bit-reproducible.
> Do not tabulate the two kinds of number together without this distinction.

## The one thread running through all of it

Four documents reached the same conclusion by independent routes, which is the
main thing this merge makes visible:

- **[snapshots/02](snapshots/02-informativeness-and-deception.md) predicted it.**
  A task is hard when partial states don't reveal their promise
  (*uninformative*) and a cheap terminal outscores the path to the optimum
  (*deceptive*). The `sine` game sits in the worst corner.
- **[snapshots/03](snapshots/03-additive-instance-results.md) refined it.** The
  prediction "a monotone ladder climbs even at 25 sims" was **wrong**.
  $V^*$-monotonicity is not sufficient; what binds is the gap between the best
  *cheap* terminal and the optimum, relative to the search budget. `sine` is a
  *deep* trap ($0.87$ vs $0.99$, uninformative); `additive_quadratic` a *shallow*
  one ($0.93$ vs $1.0$, informative) — solved at ~100 sims.
- **[notes/02 §2.3](notes/02-target-families.md#23-why-the-constant-wins)
  measured the mechanism.** Mean-aggregating random rollouts over a grammar whose
  structures mostly hit the $-1$ clip floor makes *every continuation look worse
  than quitting*. On `quad_B` the search correctly ranks doing nothing above every
  road forward. More search makes the answer **worse**, and the search is not
  malfunctioning.
- **[comments/Peter1](comments/Peter1.md), [Peter2](comments/Peter2_alphadev.md)
  named it from outside**: random rollouts fail for SR; AlphaDev's state space has
  a structural property neither SR representation satisfies.

The synthesis: *the value signal is hidden, and the failure is in the
representation and the leaf estimator, not the network or the training loop.*
Each doc is one face of that. **Note the live tension worth resolving:** notes/02
§2.3 observes the mean-vs-max split is decisive (5 averaged rollouts decay
$0.546 \to 0.165$ on `quad_A` while 5 best-of rollouts hold near $1.0$), yet
`backup_rule="max"` is still listed as untested in
[snapshots/01](snapshots/01-brainstorm-directions.md)'s highest-ROI tier.

## Conventions

- `notes/` is revised in place; `snapshots/NN-*.md` are immutable — new thinking
  goes in a new numbered file, and a prediction shown wrong is recorded, not edited
  away ([snapshots/03](snapshots/03-additive-instance-results.md) is the model).
- Every figure is produced by a script in `scripts/`, never hand-drawn, and every
  note carries a **Reproduce** appendix.
- All drivers write to `Claude-research/figures/`. Nothing writes anywhere else.
- `XX-status.md` records every material change as `YYYY-MM-DD — <change>`.
