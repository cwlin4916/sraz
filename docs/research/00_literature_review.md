## Introduction

1. This note orients the repo in the literature: for each design commitment of the engine and the game it names the closest published relatives, and it collects the published remedies for the premature convergence documented in the first training run (§7).
2. The system decomposes into five commitments, each with its own literature: grammar derivation as an MDP (§3), a terminal reward computed through an inner constant-fitting oracle (§5), a single-player AlphaZero engine (§4), a configurable value-backup rule (§7), and exploration under sparse deterministic reward (§7).
3. No published system combines all five; the closest single relatives are the Symbolic Physics Learner (grammar-as-MDP MCTS, but no learned network), SR-GPT (an AlphaZero-style learn-and-search loop, but over vocabulary tokens rather than productions), and Kammerer et al.'s exhaustive grammar enumeration (the same structure space, inner fit, and cache, but no learned search) (§1, §6).
4. The deep-symbolic-regression line of Petersen et al. is the canonical RL formulation of the identical problem — token-emitting policy, legality masking, terminal fitness through an inner fit — solved by policy gradients with no tree search; its follow-ups already name and treat the early-commitment collapse this repo reproduced (§2).
5. Grammar-constrained structure search has three parallel literatures — grammar-based equation discovery (Lagramge, ProGED), grammar-guided genetic programming (Whigham, grammatical evolution), and grammar-action program synthesis (Yin & Neubig, Bunel et al.) — and the game's legality mask has a direct ancestor in each (§3).
6. The engine descends from AlphaZero through its single-player adaptations; Ranked Reward, SP-MCTS, and the greedy-value-target analysis of Willemsen et al. are the anchors for the min-max $Q$ normalization and the mean/max/top-k/softmax backup switch (§4, §7).
7. The inner lmfit oracle is itself a studied subproblem: Levenberg–Marquardt constant fitting inside structure search is standard since Kommenda et al., and its documented failure modes — nonconvexity, ill-conditioning, overparameterization — imply the terminal reward can misrank structures, so the observed collapse need not be a pure exploration failure (§5).
8. The 14-token cap over 7 productions makes the structure space finite and small; the enumeration-plus-description-length literature supplies ground truth against which any search run can be audited, and makes the cap legible as a crude hard length prior (§6).
9. The iteration-1 collapse onto `* C1 x` has a formal diagnosis — visit-count policy targets degrade the regularized-policy-optimization solution PUCT approximates, precisely at low simulation counts — and a menu of candidate fixes spanning search-side, target-side, reward-side, and population-side interventions (§7).
10. Eureqa, AI Feynman, PySR, Operon, and SRBench define the external comparison ground any scaled-up result must eventually meet (§8); §9 states what the repo does that none of the relatives do.

Citation convention: years are the year of first public appearance (usually arXiv); the venue in parentheses is the eventual archival venue where one exists.

## 1. Nearest neighbors: MCTS and AlphaZero for symbolic regression

> **Goal.** Name the systems closest to the whole repo — tree search over expression construction with a terminal fitness reward — ordered from closest.

- **Symbolic Physics Learner** — Sun et al. 2022 (ICLR 2023), [arXiv:2205.13134](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13134). Equation discovery as MCTS over a context-free grammar: states are partial parse trees, actions are production rules, terminal reward combines data fit with a sparsity discount $\eta^n/(1+\mathrm{NRMSE})$. The closest match to the game formulation; it is what the engine wraps in AlphaZero. Differences: network-free UCT with hand-designed subtree reuse ("module transplantation"), and always expands the leftmost nonterminal, whereas here the agent also chooses the buffer position.
- **SR-GPT** — Li et al. 2024 (Expert Systems with Applications 2025), [arXiv:2401.14424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14424). The most explicit AlphaZero loop in the SR literature: a transformer serves as policy and value network inside MCTS, and search results retrain the transformer. Its gains over unguided MCTS quantify what a learned prior should buy over raw UCB. Differences: leftmost token-level expansion, no grammar-production action space, no fixed-length buffer. Its unpublished predecessor **AlphaSymbol** (LSTM-guided MCTS, [OpenReview 2023](https://openreview.net/forum?id=1iKydVG6pL)) states the same motivation: fixed pretrained policies generalize poorly, so search and learning must alternate per problem.
- **Neural-guided symbolic regression with asymptotic constraints** — Li et al. 2019 (arXiv, NeurIPS-19 KR2ML workshop), [arXiv:1901.07714](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.07714). The earliest instance of the exact action-space design: a neural network proposes CFG production rules to guide MCTS. The policy is trained supervised on synthetic expressions and conditions on desired asymptotic behavior; there is no value network or self-play.
- **TPSR** — Shojaee et al. 2023 (NeurIPS 2023), [arXiv:2303.06833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06833). MCTS as a decoding-time planner over a pretrained SR transformer: model logits as P-UCB priors, terminal reward mixing fit accuracy (after BFGS constant refinement) with a complexity penalty, caching of evaluated sequences. Matches the reward pathway here nearly one-for-one — terminal-only, computed through an inner fit, cached — but the prior is frozen pretrained knowledge, not self-play learning; the complexity term is a principled response to the fit-versus-length tension the 14-token cap creates.
- **Deep generative symbolic regression with MCTS** — Kamienny et al. 2023 (ICML 2023), [arXiv:2302.11223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11223). MCTS whose expansion is a context-aware neural mutation model refined online from successful searches; shares the thesis that neural guidance plus tree search beats either alone. Search states are complete expressions edited by mutations, not partial derivations.
- **RSRM** — Xu et al. 2023 (ICLR 2024), [arXiv:2305.14656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14656). MCTS with double Q-learning-guided expansion plus a modulated sub-tree discovery block that learns reusable expression motifs. The Q-learning targets the same exploration/exploitation balance the min-max normalization and backup switch manage; sub-tree reuse is one escape mechanism from short local optima.
- **Neural-guided equation discovery (MGMT)** — Brugger et al. 2025 (arXiv book chapter), [arXiv:2503.16953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16953). A multi-task neural-guided MCTS over a CFG that empirically studies three of this repo's live design questions: grammar-production actions beat token actions; how to train the guiding network (supervised vs RL); and exploration modifications for sparse deterministic reward, including risk-seeking MCTS and AmEx-MCTS (§7).
- **Improving MCTS for symbolic regression** — Huang et al. 2025 (NeurIPS 2025), [arXiv:2509.15929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15929). Argues that because SR seeks the single best expression rather than maximal expected return, mean-backup UCB is the wrong bandit, and replaces it with an extreme-bandit allocation with finite-time guarantees, plus evolution-inspired state-jumping actions. This is the theoretical grounding for exposing the backup rule as a config knob.
- **AlphaZero for physics** — Michishita 2023 (J. Phys. Soc. Japan 2024), [arXiv:2311.12713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12713). One of the few explicit single-player AlphaZero-for-symbolic-expressions papers (Floquet high-frequency expansions). Limited engine detail; the objective is agreement of a derived analytic expansion, not $R^2$ against data.
- **AlphaCFG** — Yang et al. 2026 (arXiv), [arXiv:2601.22119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22119). Alpha-factor discovery for quantitative finance as a "linguistic MDP" over a size-controlled CFG, searched by grammar-aware MCTS with policy/value networks — independent confirmation that the grammar-games framing generalizes beyond SR. Terminal reward is trading performance, not a fit oracle.
- **Monte-Carlo expression discovery** — Cazenave 2013 (IJAIT), [DOI:10.1142/S0218213012500352](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/abs/10.1142/S0218213012500352). The historical root: UCT and Nested Monte-Carlo Search over single-player expression construction with terminal-only evaluation, beating GP in evaluations used. Learning-free; predates grammar-as-MDP formulations.

## 2. The deep-symbolic-regression line: RL without search

> **Goal.** The canonical RL formulation of the same problem, and its own encounters with premature convergence.

- **Deep symbolic regression** — Petersen et al. 2019 (ICLR 2021 oral), [arXiv:1912.04871](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04871). An RNN policy emits expression tokens in pre-order; constants are fitted by an inner BFGS solve; the squashed NRMSE is a terminal reward optimized by a *risk-seeking* policy gradient (only the top-$\varepsilon$ quantile of sampled rewards contributes). In-situ constraints mask invalid tokens, including length bounds. This is the same problem statement as the game — token policy, legality mask, terminal fitness through an imperfect inner fit — with policy-gradient sampling in place of MCTS; risk-seeking is the policy-gradient analogue of a max-style value backup.
- **Improving exploration in policy gradient search** — Landajuela et al. 2021 (ICLR 2021 MATH-AI workshop), [arXiv:2107.09158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.09158). Names two failure modes of neural expression search — *early commitment* (premature entropy collapse on the first tokens) and *initialization bias toward short sequences* — and fixes them with a hierarchical entropy regularizer and a soft length prior. This is the closest published diagnosis of the iteration-1 collapse onto the shortest good expression; both remedies act on the sampling distribution and have no MCTS counterpart in the engine yet.
- **Neural-guided GP population seeding** — Mundhenk et al. 2021 (NeurIPS 2021), [arXiv:2111.00053](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00053). The DSR policy seeds restarted GP populations; the hybrid beats both pure RL and pure GP. The motivating observation — pure neural policy search stalls and needs an external search operator — is an empirical statement of the same plateau.
- **uDSR** — Landajuela et al. 2022 (NeurIPS 2022), [proceedings page](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/dbca58f35bddc6e4003b2dd80e42f838-Abstract-Conference.html). Hybridizes five SR strategy classes (problem simplification, neural-guided search, pretraining, GP, linear-model tokens); the ablations quantify how far the pure-RL core falls short alone — calibration for what a single minimal learner must overcome.
- **PhySO** — Tenachi et al. 2023 (ApJ), [arXiv:2303.03192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03192). DSR plus in-situ dimensional-analysis masking: tokens with inconsistent physical units are zeroed from the policy at each step. Demonstrates how much hard masking prunes under a terminal-fit reward — the units mask is the physics analogue of the grammar legality mask.
- **DySymNet** — Li et al. 2023 (ICML 2024), [arXiv:2309.13705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13705). The policy samples architectures of symbolic networks whose weights an inner SGD fit determines; same two-level structure (discrete choice above, continuous nonconvex fit below), different structure representation.
- **Sym-Q** — Tian et al. 2024 (Nature Communications 2025), [arXiv:2402.05306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05306). Offline RL: a Q-network over partial expression trees trained from large demonstration corpora — the value-learning counterpart of the value head over partial sentential forms, learned from data rather than self-play.
- **GFN-SR** — Li et al. 2023 (NeurIPS 2023 AI4Science workshop), [arXiv:2312.00396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00396). GFlowNet expression construction: sample trees with probability proportional to reward rather than maximizing. Directly targets reward-maximization mode collapse (§7).

## 3. Grammar-constrained search: equation discovery, GP, program synthesis

> **Goal.** The three literatures in which "derivation = sequence of production choices" already exists, and the ancestors of the legality mask.

**Grammar-based equation discovery.**

- **Lagramge** — Todorovski & Džeroski 1997 (ICML 1997), [author page](https://kt.ijs.si/~ljupco/papers/icml-97.html). The founding paper of the problem statement: a user-supplied CFG declaratively defines candidate equation structures, searched exhaustively or by beam search under a depth limit, each structure's constants fitted by nonlinear least squares and scored by fit error. The two-level architecture (CFG structure space above, NLS constant fit below) is exactly the game's, complexity cap included.
- **ProGED** — Brence et al. 2020 (Knowledge-Based Systems 2021), [arXiv:2012.00428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.00428). Monte Carlo sampling of complete derivations from a *probabilistic* CFG, constants fitted per candidate. Its fixed rule probabilities are precisely the non-adaptive prior the trained policy network is meant to replace.
- **Bayesian symbolic regression** — Jin et al. 2019 (arXiv), [arXiv:1910.08892](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08892). MCMC over expression structures under a conciseness prior, coefficients fitted within the sampler; structural priors play the role assigned here to the learned policy prior.
- **Bayesian machine scientist** — Guimerà et al. 2020 (Science Advances), [arXiv:2004.12157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12157). MCMC over closed-form models scored by an approximate marginal posterior with priors learned from a corpus of published equations — a natural random-walk exploration baseline against which PUCT-with-noise can be measured.

**Grammar-guided genetic programming.**

- **Grammatically-based genetic programming** — Whigham 1995 (ICML-95 GP workshop), [gpbib page](https://gpbib.cs.ucl.ac.uk/gp-html/whigham_1995_GBGP.html). The original argument for a CFG as declarative inductive bias with validity-by-construction — the property the legality mask enforces per action.
- **Grammatical evolution** — O'Neill & Ryan 2001 (IEEE TEC), [IEEE page](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/942529). Genotype codons successively select which production to apply to the leftmost nonterminal: each codon is one production decision, minus the position choice. GE's wrapping and length-limit pathologies foreshadow the buffer-cap expressibility gap.
- **Grammar-based GP: a survey** — McKay et al. 2010 (GPEM), [Springer page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10710-010-9109-y). Maps the design space of CFG-constrained search — bias encoding, validity guarantees, depth/length limits — that the game instantiates as mask plus buffer.
- **Grammar variational autoencoder** — Kusner et al. 2017 (ICML 2017), [arXiv:1703.01925](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.01925). Decodes sequences of production rules under a stack-plus-mask scheme so only valid productions can be emitted — the direct ancestor of the mask condition $\mathrm{lhs}(p_j) = s_i$; search happens in a continuous latent space rather than in the derivation MDP.

**Program synthesis with grammar actions and execution reward.**

- **A syntactic neural model for code generation** — Yin & Neubig 2017 (ACL 2017), [arXiv:1704.01696](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.01696). The canonical formalization of generation as a sequence of grammar actions (APPLYRULE on the frontier nonterminal of a growing AST) — load-bearing prior art for grammar-structured action spaces, in fixed depth-first order.
- **Leveraging grammar and RL for neural program synthesis** — Bunel et al. 2018 (ICLR 2018), [arXiv:1805.04276](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04276). The exact triad the engine embodies — grammar-derived action masking, sparse execution-based reward on complete programs, RL to optimize semantic correctness — with supervised pretraining where this repo is tabula rasa.
- **Execution-guided neural program synthesis** — Chen et al. 2019 (ICLR 2019), [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=H1gfOiAqYm). Conditions the synthesizer on intermediate execution states of the partial program; quantifies what terminal-only evaluation discards. The analogue here would be scoring partial sentential forms (e.g., fitting with unexpanded nonterminals treated as extra constants).
- **Write, execute, assess** — Ellis et al. 2019 (NeurIPS 2019), [arXiv:1906.04604](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.04604). Policy writes code, a REPL executes partial programs, a learned value assesses the state; policy and value guide sequential Monte Carlo under terminal-only reward — structurally the closest program-synthesis match to the policy/value-under-sparse-reward design.
- **AlphaNPI** — Pierrot et al. 2019 (NeurIPS 2019), [arXiv:1905.12941](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12941). Single-agent PUCT MCTS with policy/value networks and Dirichlet noise composing programs from sparse binary terminal rewards — the most direct precedent for a minimal single-player AlphaZero over symbolic construction, with hierarchical actions and a level curriculum.
- **DreamCoder** — Ellis et al. 2020 (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 2023), [arXiv:2006.08381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381). Neurally guided enumeration under a probabilistic grammar whose library *grows* by compressing recurring fragments into new primitives — the strongest precedent for the general grammar-games framing, and a pointer to what a multi-task version of this repo could become.

## 4. Single-player AlphaZero for combinatorial and mathematical discovery

> **Goal.** The engine's ancestry, and what breaks when AlphaZero leaves two-player zero-sum games.

- **AlphaZero** — Silver et al. 2017 (Science 2018), [arXiv:1712.01815](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01815). PUCT selection, Dirichlet root noise, visit-count policy targets, value bootstrapping — the engine reimplements all of it, so every deviation (min-max $Q$ normalization, backup switch, single-player subtree reuse) is defined relative to this baseline. The two-player setting supplies bounded outcomes and an implicit opponent curriculum, both replaced by hand here (reward clipping; nothing yet replaces the curriculum).
- **Expert iteration** — Anthony et al. 2017 (NeurIPS 2017), [arXiv:1705.08439](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08439). The search-as-policy-improvement-operator template of the self-play → replay → train loop; its lens — when does the search expert actually produce targets better than the current policy — is the right frame for why training stalls after the collapse.
- **SP-MCTS** — Schadd et al. 2008 (Computers and Games), [Springer chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-87608-3_1). The founding statement that in single-player games the mean of simulation returns is not the right node statistic and best-found values carry information — the pre-neural ancestor of the mean/max/top-k backup switch.
- **Ranked Reward** — Laterre et al. 2018 (NeurIPS 2018 Deep RL workshop), [arXiv:1807.01672](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01672). Single-player AlphaZero for bin packing: each episode's raw score is ranked against a percentile of recent scores and binarized to ±1, restoring a self-play dynamic against one's own past performance. Directly relevant: under Ranked Reward, `* C1 x` would stop being rewarded the moment it became typical — a moving threshold where the current engine keeps the raw deterministic $R^2$.
- **Value targets in AlphaZero (A0GB)** — Willemsen et al. 2020 (Neural Computing & Applications 2022), [Springer article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00521-021-05928-5). Analyzes value-target families — outcomes, tree means, and a greedy best-line backup — and shows mean targets are contaminated by exploratory actions. The direct literature anchor for the configurable backup rule; in a deterministic game the max backup is unbiased for the best line found (§7).
- **Gumbel AlphaZero** — Danihelka et al. 2022 (ICLR 2022 spotlight), [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=bERaNdoegnO). Proves visit-count policy targets can fail to be a policy improvement at low simulation counts and replaces root Dirichlet-PUCT with Gumbel top-k plus sequential halving, guaranteeing improvement. With 105 actions and 25 simulations, this failure mode is plausibly the mechanism of the observed collapse; a drop-in engine fix to test.
- **AlphaTensor** — Fawzi et al. 2022 (Nature), [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05172-4). The flagship single-player AlphaZero on a deterministic sparse-reward mathematical search problem, with a menu of adaptations: reward shaping by known upper bounds, a distributional value head, diversity injection via change-of-basis augmentation, synthetic demonstrations.
- **AlphaDev** — Mankowitz et al. 2023 (Nature), [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06004-9). Builds a discrete symbolic artifact token-by-token under legality constraints and a length cap, scored by an external oracle (correctness + latency) — the same structure-scored-by-oracle pattern, with partially densified per-step correctness feedback where this repo's reward is strictly terminal.
- **DeepCubeA** — Agostinelli et al. 2019 (Nature Machine Intelligence), [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-019-0070-z). The alternative to self-play exploration when a goal state is known: generate training states backwards from the goal. The analogue — training on derivations of known-good expressions — is available here because the score ladder is computable (§6).
- **FunSearch** — Romera-Paredes et al. 2023 (Nature 2024), [Nature article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6). Evolutionary search over programs sampled from a frozen LLM against a deterministic evaluator; its island-model diversity machinery is a population-level answer to single-mode collapse.
- **RL on the Andrews–Curtis conjecture** — Shehper et al. 2024 (arXiv), [arXiv:2408.15332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15332). The closest "mathematical discovery as sparse-reward token-manipulation MDP" relative: states are length-capped symbol buffers (their relator-length cap parallels the 14-token cap, and both caps make some solutions unreachable), actions are local rewrites, reward is terminal and rare. Uses PPO and classical search rather than neural MCTS.

## 5. The inner oracle: constant fitting as a studied subproblem

> **Goal.** Establish that the terminal reward's imperfection is a documented, mechanistic phenomenon — and that the collapse may be partly an oracle artifact.

- **Parameter identification for SR using nonlinear least squares** — Kommenda et al. 2020 (GPEM), [Springer article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10710-019-09371-3). The anchor for the reward path: Levenberg–Marquardt constant fitting (the algorithm family lmfit wraps) integrated into structure search, with the design knobs — iteration budget, initialization, which candidates to fit — that gate every value and policy target the engine trains on.
- **Evaluating constant-optimization methods** — de Melo et al. 2015 (BRACIS), [IEEE page](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7423910/). Even given the *correct* structure, inner optimizers frequently fail and "may guide the method towards the wrong solution." The cleanest early statement of the live hazard: the 14-token frontier structure scores $0.9873$ only if the nonconvex sine-frequency fit succeeds; in a bad basin it can rank below `* C1 x`, making the collapse a rational response to a corrupted ranking.
- **Local optimization is often ill-conditioned** — Kronberger 2022 (SYNASC), [arXiv:2209.00942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00942). SVD of fit Jacobians shows rank-deficiency and ill-conditioning are frequent — the inner solve is often structurally ill-posed, not unlucky. The grammar freely derives overparameterized forms whose fail-soft $-1$ rewards are then predictable functions of structure.
- **Reducing redundant parameters** — Kronberger & de França 2024 (J. Symbolic Computation 2025), [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747717124001172). Simplification that removes redundant parameters before fitting improves the solve — a cheap oracle-hardening step compatible with the decode-then-fit pipeline.
- **Benchmarking constant-optimization schemes** — dos Reis et al. 2024 (arXiv), [arXiv:2412.02126](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02126). No single inner optimizer dominates, and the choice changes which structures win: the lmfit default is itself a hyperparameter of the reward function.
- **Revisiting gradient-based local search** — Burlacu et al. 2025 (GPTP XXI), [Springer chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-96-0077-9_13). How much inner-fit compute is worth spending — the compute-allocation question faced at every terminal state — plus Lamarckian write-back, whose analogue is warm-starting cached constants for structurally similar sentences.
- **SAGE-Fit** — Wang et al. 2026 (arXiv), [arXiv:2605.23272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23272). Names the "Good Structure, Bad Score" bottleneck and fixes it with conditionally-linear decoupling and semantic-diversity initialization — a plug-and-play upgrade candidate for the reward oracle.
- Two transformer-era answers bracket the design space: **NeSymReS** (Biggio et al. 2021, ICML 2021, [arXiv:2106.06427](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06427)) canonizes the skeleton-then-fit protocol the game uses (structure with symbolic constants, inner BFGS/LM solve determines the score), while **end-to-end transformer SR** (Kamienny et al. 2022, NeurIPS 2022, [arXiv:2204.10532](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.10532)) is the explicit counterargument — predict constants as tokens, no inner solver — evidence on whether the nonconvex oracle is a necessary component or an avoidable bottleneck.

## 6. The bounded space: enumeration, description length, complexity

> **Goal.** The literature that treats a capped grammar space exactly — the source of ground truth for auditing search.

- **SR by exhaustive search over a grammar** — Kammerer et al. 2020 (GPTP XVII), [arXiv:2109.13895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13895). Structurally the closest match to the whole game/reward stack: a CFG defines the space, a hard length restriction bounds it, constants are fitted by NLS inside the sweep, fits are hashed and cached, and semantically equivalent structures are deduplicated. The search order is a hand-designed heuristic — the repo replaces exactly that component with a learned policy/value ordering. Their deduplication also measures how many distinct derivation paths collapse to identical expressions, a quantity relevant to the 105-action space.
- **Prioritized grammar enumeration** — Worm & Chiu 2013 (GECCO 2013), [ACM DOI](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2463372.2463486). Systematic production-rule expansion ordered by a Pareto priority queue, abstract parameters fitted by nonlinear regression, memoization ensuring each structure is evaluated once — the direct ancestor of the per-sentence fit cache.
- **Exhaustive symbolic regression** — Bartlett et al. 2022 (IEEE TEC), [arXiv:2211.11461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.11461). Enumerate all semantically distinct equations to a complexity cap, fit each, rank by minimum description length. The 14-token space is small enough for an ESR-style sweep with restarted fits, which would (i) certify the $0.9873$ surrogate as the true expressible optimum and (ii) separate exploration failure from corrupted-oracle failure in any training run.
- **SR and model selection by MDL** — Desmond 2025 (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 2026), [arXiv:2507.13033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13033). Makes the accuracy-versus-length tradeoff formal, including charging for fitted constants; the 14-token cap is legible as a crude hard length prior, and MDL is its principled replacement.
- **Priors for symbolic regression** — Bartlett et al. 2023 (GECCO 2023 companion), [arXiv:2304.06333](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06333). An n-gram prior over operator arrangements fitted from known equations — a training-free structural prior that could bias root exploration away from the trivial attractor, alongside Dirichlet noise.
- **SR is NP-hard** — Virgolin & Pissis 2022 (TMLR), [arXiv:2207.01018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01018). Justifies learned search at scale while flagging that the current instance is finite and enumerable — so the observed collapse is an exploration/target failure, not intrinsic intractability.

## 7. Escaping `* C1 x`: collapse theory and exploration mechanisms

> **Goal.** A diagnosis of the iteration-1 collapse, then the candidate fixes grouped by which engine component they modify.

**Why the collapse happens — the theory.**

- **MCTS as regularized policy optimization** — Grill et al. 2020 (ICML 2020), [arXiv:2007.12509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12509). PUCT tracks the solution of a KL-regularized policy optimization, and the visit-count distribution used as the training target is a provably degraded proxy at low simulation counts. This is the direct formal diagnosis of the observed run: the one-step expression earns a high backed-up $Q$ immediately, visits concentrate, the sharpened visit-count target feeds back into the prior, and the regularization that should keep other productions alive is exactly what the approximation loses. The proposed fix — act, search, and learn from the exact regularized solution $\bar\pi$ instead of visit counts — is a target-side intervention.
- **Convex regularization in MCTS** — Dam et al. 2020 (ICML 2021), [arXiv:2007.00391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00391). Generalizes to arbitrary convex regularizers (maximum/relative/Tsallis entropy) with convergence rates — the menu from which a principled softmax backup should be drawn rather than improvised.
- **MENTS** — Xiao et al. 2019 (NeurIPS 2019), [proceedings page](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/7ffb4e0ece07869880d51662a2234143-Abstract.html). Softmax (log-sum-exp) backups paired with an entropy-regularized selection rule, with exponential convergence of root-error probability. Implication for the engine: a softmax backup wants a matching selection rule, not plain PUCT.
- **Boltzmann exploration in MCTS (BTS/DENTS)** — Painter et al. 2023 (NeurIPS 2023), [arXiv:2404.07732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07732). Fixed-temperature softmax backups can converge to the soft-optimal rather than the true argmax; a decaying-entropy schedule preserves early exploration while recovering the optimum — the failure mode and fix the softmax backup option inherits.
- **A unified perspective on value backup and exploration** — Dam et al. 2022 (JAIR 2024), [arXiv:2202.07071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07071). The systematic treatment of the mean/max/power-mean/entropy backup axis, including when max-like backups help in deterministic settings — the backup switch, done as theory.

**Search-side fixes** (modify selection or the tree):

- **Gumbel root selection** (Danihelka et al., §4) — guaranteed policy improvement at low simulation counts; the most direct engine-level candidate.
- **AmEx-MCTS** — Derstroff et al. 2024 (arXiv), [arXiv:2402.08511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08511). Decouples selection paths, visit counts, and value backups to steer simulations away from fully-explored subtrees — in a deterministic game with cached fits, vanilla PUCT wastes simulations re-visiting the same terminal expression; this is the targeted repair.
- **Extreme-bandit allocation** (Huang et al., §1) — replace the UCB objective itself when the goal is the single best expression.

**Reward-side fixes** (modify what the game returns):

- **Ranked Reward** (Laterre et al., §4) — binarize against a moving percentile of recent scores; the incumbent stops being rewarded once typical.
- **Count-based bonuses** — Bellemare et al. 2016 (NeurIPS 2016), [arXiv:1606.01868](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01868). Pseudo-counts generalize count bonuses to large state spaces; here states are exact discrete buffers, so true counts are trivially available and a $1/\sqrt{N(s)}$ bonus explicitly pays the agent to leave the collapsed path.
- **Novelty search** — Lehman & Stanley 2011 (Evolutionary Computation), [DOI:10.1162/EVCO_a_00025](https://doi.org/10.1162/EVCO_a_00025). Frames the failure as *deception*: the objective's local gradient favors the short expression while the optimum requires low-immediate-value composition. A natural behavior descriptor exists — the fitted expression's predictions at the 41 sample points — enabling novelty or novelty-plus-$R^2$ hybrid rewards.

**Target/replay-side fixes** (modify what the network learns from):

- **Priority queue training** — Abolafia et al. 2018 (arXiv), [arXiv:1801.03526](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03526). Keep a persistent top-K buffer of best-scoring programs and add a likelihood term on it — a minimal-change mechanism to lock in rare high-$R^2$ discoveries that the recency-windowed replay currently dilutes.
- **A0GB greedy value targets** (Willemsen et al., §4) and **$\bar\pi$ policy targets** (Grill et al., above).

**Population/restart-side fixes** (modify where episodes start):

- **Go-Explore** — Ecoffet et al. 2019 (arXiv; Nature 2021 as "First return, then explore"), [arXiv:1901.10995](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.10995). Archive promising states, deterministically return, then explore. The environment is deterministic and cheaply resettable to any sentential form, so archived partial derivations (e.g., forms already containing `sin`) could seed MCTS roots or self-play starts.
- **GFlowNets** — Bengio et al. 2021 (NeurIPS 2021), [arXiv:2106.04399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04399). The motivating pathology is verbatim the observed one: "standard return maximization tends to converge to a single return-maximizing sequence." Grammar derivation is exactly a GFlowNet-style constructive MDP with deterministic terminal reward; reward-proportional sampling is the alternative objective (GFN-SR, §2, is the SR instantiation).
- **Island-model diversity** (FunSearch, §4) and **backward generation from known-good states** (DeepCubeA, §4).

## 8. Baselines and benchmarks

> **Goal.** The external comparison ground for any scaled-up version of the game.

- **Eureqa** — Schmidt & Lipson 2009 (Science), [Science DOI](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1165893). The founding modern-SR baseline; its accuracy–complexity Pareto selection contrasts with the scalar clipped-$R^2$ reward, which encodes no complexity pressure — relevant because the degenerate attractor `* C1 x` is the trivially simple end of that front.
- **AI Feynman** — Udrescu & Tegmark 2019 (Science Advances 2020), [arXiv:1905.11481](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11481). Physics-inspired recursive problem decomposition; its Feynman equation set (absorbed into SRBench) is the ground-truth-recovery target beyond the single hidden instance.
- **SRBench** — La Cava et al. 2021 (NeurIPS 2021 D&B), [arXiv:2107.14351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14351). The standard benchmark (252 problems; $R^2$-versus-complexity and exact symbolic recovery under noise); its criteria match the game's internals — test $R^2$ is the terminal reward, and symbolic recovery is the natural test of finding the generating form rather than the capped surrogate.
- **PySR** — Cranmer 2023 (arXiv), [arXiv:2305.01582](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01582). The de facto GP baseline; its evolve–simplify–optimize loop interleaves discrete search with periodic inner constant re-optimization, and it uses annealing-style acceptance against exactly this premature-convergence failure.
- **Operon** — Burlacu et al. 2020 (GECCO 2020 companion), [ACM DOI](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3377929.3398099). Fast GP with built-in Levenberg–Marquardt constant fitting — the same inner oracle, made a first-class search component; among the strongest classical performers on SRBench.
- **LLM-SR** — Shojaee et al. 2024 (ICLR 2025 oral), [arXiv:2404.18400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18400). LLMs propose equation skeletons as programs, parameters fitted against data — the current LLM paradigm keeps the same bilevel split; its experience buffer is a prompt-space analogue of the replay window.
- **LLM-SRBench** — Shojaee et al. 2025 (ICML 2025 oral), [arXiv:2504.10415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10415). Memorization-proof equation-discovery benchmark; a from-scratch RL system with a hidden target is by construction memorization-free, making the synthetic track the fairest external comparison.

## 9. Positioning

> **Goal.** State what the repo's combination does that none of the relatives do, and where each commitment has its anchor.

| Design commitment | Nearest anchors | What differs here |
|---|---|---|
| CFG productions as MDP actions | Symbolic Physics Learner; Li et al. 2019; grammatical evolution; Yin & Neubig | The action is a *(position, production)* pair — the agent chooses **where** to rewrite; every anchor expands the leftmost/frontier nonterminal in fixed order |
| Fixed 15-slot buffer, 14-token cap | Lagramge depth limits; Kammerer length restrictions; GE wrapping | The cap deliberately excludes the generating form, making "best expressible surrogate" the object of study rather than a nuisance |
| Terminal $R^2$ after inner lmfit | Lagramge; Kommenda; ProGED; TPSR; NeSymReS | Clipped to $[-1,1]$, fail-soft, cached per sentence, deterministic by default — a noise-free value-target regime none of the anchors isolate |
| Single-player AlphaZero self-play loop | AlphaNPI; Ranked Reward; SR-GPT; Michishita | Tabula rasa (no pretraining, demonstrations, or GP hybrid) on a per-problem game, with min-max $Q$ normalization replacing two-player value bounds |
| Configurable backup rule (mean/max/top-k/softmax) | SP-MCTS; A0GB; MENTS; Dam et al. | Exposed as a first-class experimental axis in a deterministic-reward game, where the theory says the choice matters most |

Three summary observations.

1. **The combination is unoccupied.** Grammar-production actions with agent-chosen positions, inside a from-scratch single-player AlphaZero, on a deterministic cached fit-oracle reward: each pairwise combination exists (SPL has the grammar without learning; SR-GPT has the learning without the grammar; Kammerer has the space and the oracle without either), but no published system holds all of it at once.
2. **The instance is auditable.** Because the capped space is finite and the reward deterministic, an exhaustive sweep (§6) can produce the exact structure ranking — so exploration failures, value-target failures, and oracle failures are separable here in a way they are not in any of the benchmark-scale relatives. That auditability, not benchmark performance, is the near-term scientific asset.
3. **The collapse is the expected failure, with an established treatment menu.** The iteration-1 convergence onto `* C1 x` matches the low-simulation visit-count pathology of Grill et al., the early-commitment diagnosis of Landajuela et al., and the deception framing of Lehman & Stanley; the fixes divide cleanly by engine component (§7), which is a ready-made experimental program.
